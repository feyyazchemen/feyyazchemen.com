THE EXPERIENCE

A performance where water, color, projection, and the body develop together in real time.

Feyyaz creates Ebru—the centuries-old art of painting on water—live at the center of the room. Its changing surface is carried into the space through light and projection, while dancer Celeste Gaiera responds through movement. The result is not a stage show placed beside the evening; it is an atmosphere made within it.

THE ARTISTS

Two practices.
One live work.

Feyyaz Chemen

Feyyaz is an Ebru artist and founder of OX Marbling. His work extends the tradition of water marbling into live environments through color, projection, sound, and technology.

Celeste Gaiera

Celeste is a professional ballerina and collaborative performer. Her poised, expressive movement gives the changing visual world a human presence.
